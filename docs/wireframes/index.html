📊 Project Overview

A specialized South African recruitment ATS (Applicant Tracking System) built for recruitment agencies targeting the Cape Town market. Features include AI-powered resume parsing with SA localization, job-candidate matching, POPIA compliance, and Employment Equity reporting against 18 sector-specific targets mandated by the SA EE Act.
